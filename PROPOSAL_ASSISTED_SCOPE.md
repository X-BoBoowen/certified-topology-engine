# Proposal-Assisted Scope

The engine does not discover loops. A circle proposal is accepted only after exact pairwise embedded/disjoint validation and exact per-cell factor verification `F_c = h_c G`, where `G` is the product of the proposed circle equations and every multiplier `h_c` is interval-certified strictly positive. This proves the field zero set equals the proposed link. Reach is then certified by the frozen Phase C.1 exact-input restricted oracle.
