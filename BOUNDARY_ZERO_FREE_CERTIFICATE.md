# Boundary Zero-Free Certificate

For every outer-domain cell edge, the bivariate polynomial is restricted exactly to a rational univariate polynomial. Exact endpoint evaluation rejects boundary endpoint roots. A rational Sturm chain counts distinct roots on the open segment. The segment is certified zero-free iff both endpoints are nonzero and the Sturm root count is zero. This removes the interval-dependency failure in the previous whole-edge natural interval evaluation.
